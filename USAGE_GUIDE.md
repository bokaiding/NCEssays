# NCEA Master - User Guide

Welcome to **NCEA Master**, your personal AI tutor for NCEA English (Levels 1, 2, 3, and Scholarship). This guide will show you how to use the platform to practice, get marked, and improve your grades.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Accessing the Platform
Once the application is running (see `README.md` for installation), open your web browser and go to:
`http://localhost:3000`

### 2. The Dashboard
When you first arrive, you will see the **Dashboard**.
*   **Select Level**: Choose Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, or Scholarship from the dropdown menu.
*   **Select Standard**: Based on your level, choose the specific achievement standard you want to practice (e.g., *AS91108 - Unfamiliar Texts*).
*   **Progress Trackers**: You will see your confidence score and past attempts here once you start using the app.

---

## 📝 Feature 1: Generating Questions (Study Mode)

This feature creates practice questions tailored to your selected standard.

### How to use it:
1.  Navigate to the **"Generate Question"** tab.
2.  **For Unfamiliar Texts**:
    *   **Option A**: Paste a news article, poem, or speech into the text box.
    *   **Option B**: Click "Use Sample Text" to let the AI provide a text for you.
    *   Select the framework you want to practice:
        *   **PETAL**: Point, Evidence, Technique, Analysis, Link (Great for paragraphs).
        *   **CAPTE**: Context, Audience/Purpose, Point, Technique, Effect (Great for holistic analysis).
3.  **For Writing Portfolios**:
    *   Select a genre (e.g., Creative Writing, Formal Essay).
    *   Enter a topic or theme (e.g., "Identity," "Justice").
4.  Click **"Generate Question"**.
5.  The AI will present a specific NCEA-style question. Read it carefully.

---

## ✍️ Feature 2: Answering & AI Marking

This is the core feature. The AI acts as a **NZQA Senior Marker** to grade your work.

### Step-by-Step:
1.  **Write your response** in the text editor provided.
    *   *Tip*: Treat this like a real exam. Manage your time and aim for the depth required for your level.
2.  Click **"Submit for Marking"**.
3.  **Wait a few seconds** while the AI analyzes your response against the official NZQA marking schedule.

### Understanding Your Results:
The AI will provide a detailed report containing:
*   **Predicted Grade**: Not Achieved, Achieved, Merit, or Excellence.
*   **Justification**: Why you received this grade, referencing specific criteria (e.g., "You showed perceptive understanding but lacked justified analysis").
*   **Strengths**: What you did well.
*   **Areas for Improvement**: Specific advice on how to reach the next grade level.
*   **Exemplar Comparison**: A button to **"View Excellence Exemplar"**. Click this to see an AI-generated answer that would get full marks for the same question. Compare your structure and vocabulary to the exemplar.

---

## 🎓 Scholarship English Mode

If you select **Scholarship**:
*   The questions will be more abstract and require critical thinking.
*   The marking criteria will focus on **coherence, perception, and originality** rather than just structural correctness.
*   Feedback will challenge your arguments and suggest deeper cultural or literary connections.

---

## 💡 Tips for Success

| Goal | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Moving from A to M** | Focus on explaining *how* the technique creates meaning, not just identifying it. |
| **Moving from M to E** | Make "perceptive" connections. Link the text to wider contexts, authorial intent, or human experience. |
| **Writing Portfolio** | Ensure your voice is consistent. Use the AI feedback to check if your tone matches the purpose. |
| **Unfamiliar Texts** | Practice the **CAPTE** framework to ensure you don't miss the context or audience purpose. |

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting

*   **"API Error"**: If you see an error when marking, the server might be busy. Wait a moment and try again.
*   **"Generic Feedback"**: If the feedback feels too general, try providing a longer response (at least 2-3 paragraphs for essays).
*   **Wrong Standard**: Ensure you selected the correct AS number (e.g., AS91108 vs AS91107) before generating, as the marking rubrics differ.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Data
*   Your answers are saved to your local dashboard to track progress.
*   No personal data is sent to third parties other than the secure AI processing endpoint.
*   Do not input real names or sensitive personal information in your practice essays.
