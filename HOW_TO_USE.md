# 🚀 How to Use NCEssays - Instant NCEA English Practice

## ⚡ Start Practicing in 5 Seconds (No Login Required!)

### **Step 1: Open the Website**
Simply navigate to your deployed NCEssays URL (e.g., `https://ncessays.yourschool.ac.nz` or `http://localhost:3000` if running locally).

**That's it!** You'll immediately see the homepage with level selection buttons.

---

## 📝 Quick Start Guide

### **For Students:**

#### **Option A: Guided Practice (Recommended)**
1. **Click your level** (Level 1, 2, 3, or Scholarship) on the homepage
2. **Choose a text type** (or click "Surprise Me" for random):
   - Poetry
   - Prose (Fiction)
   - Non-Fiction Articles
   - Media Texts (Advertisements, Speeches)
   - Visual Texts (Posters, Cartoons)
3. **Read the provided text** - already loaded on the screen!
4. **Answer the question** in the text box below
5. **Click "Get AI Feedback"** 
6. **Review your results:**
   - Predicted grade (Not Achieved/Achieved/Merit/Excellence)
   - Specific feedback on what you did well
   - Exactly what you need to improve for the next grade level
   - Compare with an Excellence exemplar response

#### **Option B: Random Quick Practice**
1. Click **"Start Random Practice"** button on homepage
2. Get instantly matched with a random text + question
3. Write your response and get feedback

#### **Option C: Study Mode (Step-by-Step Learning)**
1. Select your level, then click **"Study Mode"**
2. The AI breaks down the question using PETAL/CAPTE framework:
   - **P**oint - What's your main argument?
   - **E**vidence - Which quotes support it?
   - **T**echnique - What language features are used?
   - **A**nalysis - How does this create meaning?
   - **L**ink - Connect back to the question
3. Answer each step individually
4. Get feedback on each component before writing your full response

## 🎯 Understanding Your Feedback

When you submit your answer, you'll receive:

### **Grade Prediction**
- **Not Achieved (N)**: Doesn't meet criteria yet
- **Achieved (A)**: Meets basic requirements
- **Merit (M)**: Shows insightful understanding
- **Excellence (E)**: Demonstrates perceptive, sophisticated analysis

### **Feedback Sections**
✅ **Strengths**: What you did well (using NCEA terminology)
⚠️ **Areas for Improvement**: Specific gaps preventing the next grade
📊 **Breakdown**: Score by criterion (understanding, analysis, evidence, structure)
💡 **Next Steps**: Actionable tips to improve
🏆 **Exemplar Comparison**: Side-by-side with an Excellence response

## 💡 Pro Tips for Better Grades

### **Moving from Achieved → Merit:**
- Add **specific evidence** (quotes with line numbers)
- Explain **how** techniques create meaning, not just identify them
- Structure paragraphs logically (one idea per paragraph)

### **Moving from Merit → Excellence:**
- Show **perceptive understanding** (multiple layers of meaning)
- Make **connections** between different parts of the text
- Discuss **author's purpose** and wider context
- Use **sophisticated vocabulary** and varied sentence structures

### **Scholarship Success:**
- Develop an **original thesis** (don't just repeat obvious ideas)
- Synthesize **multiple texts** or perspectives
- Demonstrate **critical evaluation** (question assumptions)
- Write with **voice and style** (engage the reader)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### **Is this official NZQA material?**
No. This is a **practice tool** using original, copyright-safe content aligned to NCEA standards. Always prioritize your teacher's feedback and official NZQA resources for actual assessments.

### **How accurate is the AI marking?**
The AI is trained on NZQA marking schedules and provides consistent, criteria-based feedback. However, it's not a replacement for human markers. Use it for **formative practice**, not summative assessment.

### **Can I save my progress?**
Currently, sessions are temporary. For tracking progress over time, consider:
- Taking screenshots of feedback
- Keeping a reflection journal
- Using the "Export Feedback" button (if available in your deployment)

### **What if I don't agree with the AI's grade?**
Great! That's critical thinking. Compare the feedback against:
- Official NZQA marking schedules
- Your teacher's expectations
- Exemplar responses provided
Discuss discrepancies with your teacher.

### **Is my writing stored or shared?**
No. Responses are processed in real-time and not permanently stored (unless your school has enabled analytics). Check your institution's privacy policy.

### **Can I use this on my phone?**
Yes! The site is fully responsive and works on mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.

### **How many times can I practice?**
Unlimited! There are no usage limits for basic practice. The AI generator can create infinite new texts.

## 🎓 Level-Specific Expectations

### **Level 1**
- **Response length**: 1-2 well-developed paragraphs
- **Focus**: Identify techniques, explain basic effects
- **Evidence**: 2-3 relevant quotes
- **Goal**: Show clear understanding of the text

### **Level 2**
- **Response length**: 2-3 developed paragraphs
- **Focus**: Analyze how techniques create meaning
- **Evidence**: 3-4 integrated quotes with analysis
- **Goal**: Show insightful understanding of author's purpose

### **Level 3**
- **Response length**: 3-4 sophisticated paragraphs
- **Focus**: Evaluate effectiveness, discuss multiple interpretations
- **Evidence**: 4-5+ quotes woven into analysis
- **Goal**: Show perceptive, nuanced understanding

### **Scholarship**
- **Response length**: Cohesive essay (400-600 words)
- **Focus**: Original thesis, critical evaluation, synthesis
- **Evidence**: Strategic quote selection supporting unique argument
- **Goal**: Demonstrate exceptional insight and articulate expression
